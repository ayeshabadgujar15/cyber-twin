One-time setup for this local app

The Google Cloud project owner needs to enable Gmail API and download an OAuth client of type Desktop app. Choose that JSON file below. It stays on this computer; no callback URLs or port edits are needed for Desktop clients.

  1. Open Google Cloud → Google Auth Platform → Clients → Create client.
  2. Choose Desktop app, create it and download its JSON file.
  3. If the app is in Testing, add your Gmail address under Audience → Test users.

Understand the evidence

Dates come from email metadata. A subject may describe an account creation, login, security, password, or billing event. It does not verify ownership, a successful session, a charge, or a current subscription. OTPs and failed attempts never count as confirmed logins.

Review accounts safely

Open a service using its app or a saved bookmark. Check security activity and recovery settings there. Cyber Twin does not follow email links, change passwords, cancel subscriptions, or close accounts.

Coverage and privacy

Mailbox limits, deleted messages and unsupported wording can leave gaps. Missing email is not proof of inactivity. Results are normalized metadata held in memory. Background results expire after ten minutes and are cleared after the dashboard receives them. Your review marks stay in this page only.

Connection lifetime

Restarting the server clears the connection. Google testing permissions may expire after seven days. “Disconnect locally” clears this app’s credentials, scan results and pending connection attempt; it does not revoke Google’s account permission.
